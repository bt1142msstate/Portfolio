Policy

Privacy, without the fine-print maze.

This portfolio is an informational website. It does not run first-party analytics, advertising, account registration, or contact forms.

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the public portfolio at brandontemple.com, including its HTML resume, downloadable resume, project media, and policy pages. It does not govern websites reached through external links.

Information this site does not collect

  • No account registration, contact form, newsletter, or payment form is provided.
  • No first-party analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral tracking, or marketing profiles are used.
  • No first-party cookies, local storage, or session storage are used to identify or track visitors.

If those practices change, this policy will be updated before new data collection is introduced.

Hosting and technical request data

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser or device details, requested pages, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the service. Brandon Temple does not maintain a visitor-level analytics database for this portfolio.

GitHub's handling of this information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Fonts and external services

The site requests font files from Google Fonts. That request may provide Google with ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and browser details. Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Indeed, Instagram, Reddit, project websites, and technical references open services governed by their own privacy policies. Those services may collect information after you choose to visit them.

Email, downloads, and project captures

Selecting an email link opens your email application. No message or address is transmitted to Brandon Temple until you choose to send an email.

Resume and image downloads are handled by your browser. The site does not receive a notification that you downloaded a file.

Scrollable project captures are static, public-safe demonstrations hosted on this domain. They do not require sign-in and should not be used to submit personal information.

Retention and security

Because the portfolio does not directly collect visitor submissions or analytics, Brandon Temple does not retain a portfolio visitor database. Infrastructure providers may retain technical records according to their own security, legal, and retention practices.
