About

Preparing and empowering students, families, and target neighborhoods.

On April 10, 2019, Afternoon Adventure Learning Center was established as a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit organization.

The mission is to help improve the lives and education of at-risk students in grades K-12 who live in low-income areas, while supporting and providing resources to their families. The center promotes academic enrichment, health and wellness, financial literacy, job readiness, and social engagement.

Afternoon Adventure is preparing and empowering target neighborhoods for leadership in their communities and success in their personal lives.

The center is centrally located at 306 North Main Street in Downtown Newton, a family-friendly area close to Newton Elementary School, Newton High School, and the Newton Public Library. Newton Elementary is about a 5-minute drive away, Newton High is about a 7-minute drive away, and the library is about a 3-minute drive away. Bus transportation is available for students attending Newton Municipal School District schools. Families of students attending other schools are responsible for arranging transportation.

Leadership

Jordan Ellis, Executive Director

Jordan Ellis graduated from Mississippi State University with a degree in Accounting in 2003 and has held a Certified Mississippi Teacher license since 2008. She has worked in the public school system for over 15 years, teaching English, Math, and Special Education.

Kristie's interest in afterschool programs began when her own son attended afterschool and needed that one-on-one, small group learning environment. She is passionate about creating enriching and supportive learning experiences for all children.

Her favorite thing about what she does is developing relationships with the children that she teaches and their families. She believes that small class sizes and the development of a strong community allow her and her team to really know each child as an individual and to provide a solid and supportive foundation in the early years.

All teachers are CPR certified, have undergone a background check, received health and safety training, and hold a degree in Elementary Education, either an AA or BS, and/or hold a Mississippi Certified Teacher's license.

Programs

Five major components built for students and families.

Afternoon Adventure Learning Center's program was developed to teach high-risk, inner-city youth in grades K-12 and families the skills to become successful in life. Families comparing care options can review the program details below.

Academic Enrichment

Afterschool and summer tutoring designed to improve academic, social, and personal development through one-on-one tutoring, small-group tutoring, homework help, enrichment and STEM activities, test preparation, mentoring and coaching, and parent engagement.

“Enrichment beyond the classroom”

Health & Wellness

Exercise and physical activity classes, nutrition education, stress reduction and mental health resources, health screenings, community-based events, and daily healthy snacks, milk, and juice to help close the gap between lunch and dinner.

Financial Literacy

Budgeting and saving classes, debt management, investing and retirement planning, financial counseling, and consumer protection classes to help families make informed financial decisions.

Job Readiness

Job search skills, career exploration, workplace readiness, professional development, entrepreneurship training, job placement assistance, job fairs, high school diploma and GED preparation, college preparation, and financial aid help.

Social Engagement

Peer support groups, cultural and arts programs, community outreach, volunteer opportunities, cooking classes, celebrations, social events, Toys for Tots, Coats 4 Kids, back-to-school drives, and community support.

Afterschool & Summer Program

Prepare your child for the future. Success starts here.

Afternoon Adventure supports students during the school year and through summer with homework help, academic tutoring, reading and math instruction, enrichment activities, and personalized support in a fun, safe learning environment.

  • After-school homework help and academic tutoring
  • Summer reading and math support

GED prep & support

GED help, tutoring, and youth workforce support.

Take the next step toward your future with GED help and support. Afternoon Adventure connects older students with guidance, study resources, test preparation, and encouragement to help them earn a high school equivalency diploma and move toward their education and career goals.

The supporting flyer also includes youth workforce resources such as tutoring, job search support, work ethics training, paid work experience, life skills, transportation, childcare assistance, and other participation support.
