Attendance rule book

Review before using attendance tools.

These rules keep sign-in and sign-out records consistent with how students arrive and leave the center.

Rules updated July 6, 2026 · Version sample-record

  • Daily sign-outParents, guardians, or authorized representatives physically sign children out every day.
  • Parent drop-offIf a parent or guardian drops a child off, that parent or guardian should physically sign the child in.
  • Staff check-in limitsStaff check-in is only for school bus arrivals or another approved non-parent handoff where the parent or guardian did not drop the child off.
  • Digital staff signatureWhen staff use the check-in button, the system records that staff member's account as the digital staff signature for the check-in event.
  • Arrival record detailsStaff check-in records include the attendance date, time, staff signer, selected arrival method, and any note entered for the arrival.
  • Future guardian e-signaturesParent/guardian electronic sign-in or sign-out may use a similar logged signature flow after that process is approved and enabled. Until then, parent/guardian signatures remain physical/manual.
